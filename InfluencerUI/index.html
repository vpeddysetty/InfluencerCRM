Tejdux — influencer marketing CRM

Tejdux is a business tool for brands and marketing agencies managing paid partnerships with social media creators. Import your creator list, run campaigns, attribute sales to individual creators, and pay them — from first outreach to final payout, on one board.

It is operated by KMPS Global Corporation (d/b/a Tejdux), an Ohio corporation. Signing in with Google or Facebook is offered so customers can access their own Tejdux account without a separate password; Tejdux is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of either company.

What it does

  • Import a creator roster from CSV, with a mapping preview before anything saves
  • Track every creator relationship across a drag-and-drop workflow board
  • Attribute sales to individual creators through coupon codes
  • Calculate and record what each creator earned
