2. If you have an InfluencerCRM account

Email us and we will delete your account and the data associated with it.

  1. Send an email to privacy@tejdux.com from the address your account uses. Sending from the registered address is how we confirm the request is yours — a deletion request we cannot attribute is one we must refuse.
  2. Use the subject line Data deletion request.
  3. Tell us whether you want your entire account deleted, or only the data obtained from a specific connected provider. Both are available; they are described in section 5.

We will confirm receipt and tell you what will be deleted before we act, so a request made in error can be withdrawn.

If you own a workspace, deletion affects more than you. Deleting an owner account can remove the workspace and the campaign, creator and payout records it contains, including data your colleagues rely on. We will tell you what is in scope and ask you to confirm, or help you transfer ownership first.

3. If you signed in with Facebook or Google

When you sign in through a provider, we receive your name, email address and that provider's account identifier. We do not receive or store your password, and we do not post anything on your behalf.

3.1 Removing the connection only

You can disconnect the provider without deleting your InfluencerCRM account. For Facebook:

  1. Go to Settings & Privacy → Settings in Facebook.
  2. Select Apps and Websites.
  3. Find InfluencerCRM and select Remove.

This revokes our access to your Facebook profile and removes the link between your Facebook account and ours. Your InfluencerCRM account and its workspace data remain, and you will need another way to sign in — a password, or another connected provider.

Removing the app is not the same as deleting your account.

We treat it as "stop using Facebook to sign in", not "delete everything", because for a brand owner those are very different intentions and only one of them is reversible. If you want your account deleted as well, follow section 2 — or say so in the same email and we will do both.

3.2 Deleting the data we received from the provider

To have the profile data we obtained from Facebook or Google erased, email privacy@tejdux.com with the subject Data deletion request and name the provider. We will delete the stored provider identifier, the profile details received from it, and the connection itself.

4. If a brand added you as a creator

You may be in our systems without ever having signed up, because a brand added you to their workspace or imported a spreadsheet containing your details.

In that situation the brand — not us — decides what happens to that data; we hold it on their behalf. If you know which brand it is, contact them first. They can delete your record directly and are best placed to explain why they held it.

If you do not know which brand holds your data, or a brand does not respond, email privacy@tejdux.com. We will identify the relevant brand or brands and forward your request, and we will assist them in responding. Where we are legally permitted to act directly, we will.

5. What gets deleted, and what does not

DataOn an account deletion request
Your user record — name, email, sign-in credentialsDeleted
Connected provider identifiers and profile data (Facebook, Google)Deleted
Sessions and access tokensRevoked immediately, then deleted
Workspace content owned by your account — campaigns, creator records, pagesDeleted, unless ownership is transferred first
Billing, tax and transaction records Retained where law requires, typically 6–7 years
Security and access logs Retained for 30 days, then deleted on the normal cycle
Backups Overwritten on the backup cycle, within 7 days

We keep records we are legally required to keep — chiefly financial and tax records for completed transactions. Retaining these is an obligation, not a choice, and they are not used for any other purpose after your account is deleted.

6. How long it takes

We acknowledge requests within 5 business days and complete them within 30 days, which is the limit set by the GDPR and comparable laws. Complex requests may take longer; if so, we will tell you why and give a date before the 30 days elapse.

Deletion from live systems happens first. Removal from backups follows the backup rotation and completes within 7 days. Data awaiting rotation is not accessible in the product and is not used for anything.

7. Contact

Data deletion requests: privacy@tejdux.com

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your request, you may complain to your local data protection authority. Doing so does not affect any other rights you have.
