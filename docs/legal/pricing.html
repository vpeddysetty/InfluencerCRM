What Tejdux does not do

Worth knowing before you sign up, because finding out afterwards wastes your time:

  • No creator discovery database. Tejdux manages creators you already know or found elsewhere. It will not search a catalogue of influencers for you.
  • No follower-authenticity scoring. The data needed to judge this honestly is not available from the platforms, so we do not estimate it.
  • No social publishing. Tejdux tracks the relationship and the results; it does not post on your behalf.

Billing questions

Can I cancel?

Yes, from inside the app, without contacting anyone. You keep the time you already paid for — cancelling on day 2 of a month leaves the other 28 days intact. There is no minimum term and no cancellation fee.

What happens when the Agency trial ends?

The trial runs 30 days. It converts to a paid subscription when it ends, and you are emailed before that happens. Cancel at any point during the trial and you are not charged.

What if I go over a limit?

You are stopped from adding more of that one thing — creators, brands, seats or pages — and everything already in the account keeps working. Nothing is deleted and nobody is locked out.

What happens if I downgrade?

Your data stays. If you end up over a limit you keep what exists but cannot add more until you are back under it. Team members keep the roles they already hold.

Do you offer refunds?

Email support@tejdux.com and we will sort it out. We would rather refund someone than argue with them.
