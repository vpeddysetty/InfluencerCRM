Pricing

Early access — pricing is being set with our first customers.

Tejdux is in early access, and prices are not set yet.

We are working with a small number of brands and agencies to shape the product before deciding what it costs. That is a deliberate order: a price set before anyone has run a real campaign on the tool is a guess, and it tends to be the kind of guess that is hard to walk back.

During this period there is no charge, and no card is asked for. Everyone in early access has the full product — unlimited brands, creators, seats and landing pages — because a constraint invented before there is a plan to sell would only get in the way of finding out whether the thing works.

If you are running influencer campaigns

We would like to talk to you, particularly if you are an agency handling several clients at once. Write to hello@tejdux.com and tell us roughly how many creators and brands you work with.

When pricing arrives

It will be published on this page in full — every price, no demo call required to see a number. Anyone in early access will be told before anything changes, and nothing will start charging without an explicit decision to subscribe.
