WHAT WE ARE
Tejdux (https://www.tejdux.com) is an influencer-marketing CRM for small brands. Brands use it
to manage the creators they work with, run campaigns, and publish campaign landing pages. We are
pre-launch: a small number of accounts, no public launch yet.

HOW OFTEN WE SEND, AND WHAT VOLUME
Low and event-driven. We expect well under 1,000 messages per month initially, and there is no
scheduled or campaign-style send at all. Every message is triggered by a specific action taken by
a specific person, so volume tracks signups and activity rather than a mailing schedule. Our
current 200/day sandbox quota has never been approached; we have sent 1 message in the last 24
hours.

WHAT WE SEND, AND TO WHOM
All mail is transactional. There are five kinds:

1. Account email to a person who signed up on tejdux.com: email-address verification, password
   reset, subscription and payment notifications, and account-closure confirmation.
2. Team invitations, sent only to an address that a signed-in account owner typed into our
   application in order to invite a colleague into their own workspace.
3. Collaboration email to a creator whom a brand has explicitly invited to co-author a campaign
   page: the invitation itself, and a notification when that page is published.
4. Service notices to an account owner, for example a warning before hosting for one of their
   published pages expires.
5. Operational digests to our own staff addresses.

We do not send marketing, newsletters, promotional offers, or bulk mail of any kind.

HOW WE MAINTAIN RECIPIENT LISTS
We do not maintain mailing lists, and this is the most important point about our sending model.
There is no list to subscribe to and no audience to segment. A recipient exists in our system
only because they created an account, or because an account holder deliberately entered their
address to invite them into a specific workspace or onto a specific campaign. We have never
purchased, rented, imported, or scraped a contact list, and we have no feature that would allow a
customer to upload one for emailing.

Addresses are verified at the point of use: a new account must confirm its email address via a
single-use link before the account is usable, so a mistyped or invalid address is caught
immediately rather than being mailed repeatedly.

HOW WE MANAGE BOUNCES AND COMPLAINTS
- SES account-level suppression is enabled for both BOUNCE and COMPLAINT, so an address that
  hard-bounces or complains is suppressed automatically and we do not send to it again.
- We send through a configuration set named "influencrm-prod" with reputation metrics enabled,
  so bounce and complaint rates are tracked.
- Because our mail is triggered by user actions rather than by a scheduled send, a bounced
  address does not accumulate repeat attempts: there is no retry loop and no recurring campaign
  that would re-send to a failing address.
- Our suppression list is currently empty, as we have not yet sent to real customers.

HOW RECIPIENTS UNSUBSCRIBE OR STOP RECEIVING MAIL
Because every message is transactional and tied to an account or to an explicit invitation, there
is no marketing subscription to opt out of. Recipients stop receiving mail by:
- Closing their account in the application, which stops all account email; or
- Declining or ignoring an invitation, which is never re-sent automatically; or
- Requesting deletion via our published process at https://tejdux.com/data-deletion/, or by
  emailing privacy@tejdux.com. Those requests are recorded and actioned, and our privacy policy
  at https://tejdux.com/privacy/ documents the process. Our terms are at
  https://tejdux.com/terms/.

EXAMPLES OF THE EMAIL WE SEND
Two representative examples, both plain and short:

  Subject: Confirm your email address
  Body:    Welcome to Tejdux. Please confirm your email address to finish setting up your
           account: [single-use link, expires in 24 hours]. If you did not create a Tejdux
           account, you can ignore this message.

  Subject: You have been invited to collaborate on a campaign page
  Body:    [Brand name] has invited you to help write their campaign page "[page name]".
           Open the page: [single-use link]. If you were not expecting this, you can ignore
           this message and no page will be shared with you.

Neither carries images, tracking pixels, or promotional content.
